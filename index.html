Rankings

Record and last week's rank are optional. Use NR for newly ranked teams. Arrows, colors and the biggest riser and faller fill in on their own. Works for any count: a Top 25, a 16-team conference, a Top 10.

Logos (optional)

Each team shows a badge in its school colors until you give it a logo. The logo takes the badge's spot next to the name, sized and lined up for you.

  • One team: tap the team in the preview and pick the image.
  • On a computer: drag a logo onto the team's row in the preview.
  • A bunch at once: drop them all on the preview or use the button below. Files are matched by name, so michigan_st_logo.png finds Michigan State.

Logos are saved in this browser and stay through every week's list.

Weekly routine

  1. Open this page and pick a template (your choice is remembered).
  2. Update the week tag, then edit the list: move teams, update records, set last week's rank.
  3. Tap Save PNG, or Open image to save and long-press it to save to Photos.
  4. Post it. Feed size for the grid, Story size for stories.
